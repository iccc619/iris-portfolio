Frequently Asked Questions

How many items are in total?

23 items all with various effects are available for players to pick up in the tracks. Try your best to hit the double question boxes so that you can get 2 items at the same time.

Is it equal chance to get defferent items?

Actually not very equal chance. If you are top in the race, it is less possible to get strong items like the bullet and the eight. However, if you fall behind other players, you can earn a higher chance to grab a stronger item.

Is there any items that allow me to affect other players?

Of course, Items like the shells, banana and blooper can all affect other players. And blooper and the lightening can slow down all other players all at the same time.

Which is most powerful item?

That is diiferent to diferent people. But in general the star and bullet is super powerful for players falling behind to race to a higher racking. Also, the eight is great for you to race faster with eight items at the same time.
