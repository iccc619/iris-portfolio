Frequently Asked Questions

What are the different speed classes?

50cc is the slowest and best for beginners, while 200cc is the fastest and most challenging. 100cc and 150cc offer balanced gameplay for intermediate players.

How many cups are there in total?

There are 12 cups in total, including the base game cups and DLC cups. Each cup contains 4 tracks.

What are the different types of tracks?

Tracks are divided into three categories: Nitro tracks (new to Mario Kart 8), Retro tracks (from previous games), and DLC tracks (from the Booster Course Pass).

How do I unlock all tracks?

Most tracks are available from the start. DLC tracks can be unlocked by purchasing the Booster Course Pass or completing specific challenges.

What's the difference between cups?

Each cup has its own theme and difficulty level. Some cups feature tracks from specific Nintendo franchises, while others focus on particular racing styles or environments.
